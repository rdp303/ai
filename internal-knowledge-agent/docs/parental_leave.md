# Parental Leave Policy

Eligible full-time employees receive **12 weeks of paid parental leave** following the birth, adoption, or placement of a child.

## Eligibility

Employees must have completed at least 90 days of employment before the leave start date. The policy applies equally to birth parents, non-birth parents, and adoptive parents.

## Timing

Parental leave should normally be taken within 12 months of the child's birth or placement. Employees may take the leave continuously or, with manager and People Team approval, in up to two separate blocks.

## Request process

Employees should notify their manager and submit a leave request to the People Team at least 30 days before the expected start date when practicable. The People Team confirms eligibility and coordinates payroll and benefits.

Health-plan coverage continues during approved paid parental leave under the same employee contribution terms that apply while actively working.
