# Contractor Provisioning Runbook

Contractors must have an approved sponsor and an end date before accounts are provisioned.

## Process

1. The hiring manager submits the contractor request with the contractor's legal name, company, start date, end date, sponsor, and systems required.
2. Procurement confirms that the applicable vendor or staffing agreement is active.
3. The hiring manager's department approves the requested access.
4. IT creates the identity account with the contractor classification and expiration date.
5. Application owners approve access to restricted systems before IT assigns those entitlements.
6. The sponsor confirms access on the contractor's first day.

## Timing

Submit routine contractor requests at least **three business days before the start date**. Requests involving production systems, finance data, or customer data may require Security review and additional lead time.

## Offboarding

The identity account automatically expires on the recorded end date. Sponsors must submit an extension before that date if the engagement continues. Sponsors should notify IT immediately when a contractor ends early.
