# Vendor Approval Policy

All new vendors must be reviewed before a purchase order or contract is executed.

## Approval thresholds

- **Under $5,000 annual commitment:** budget owner approval.
- **$5,000 to $24,999:** budget owner plus department VP approval.
- **$25,000 to $99,999:** budget owner, department VP, and Procurement approval.
- **$100,000 or more:** budget owner, department VP, Procurement, and Finance approval.

A **$20,000 vendor therefore requires approval from the budget owner and the department VP**.

## Additional review

Security review is required when a vendor will process confidential company or customer data. Legal review is required before signing a vendor's non-standard contract terms.

Purchase commitments must not be split into smaller transactions to avoid an approval threshold.
